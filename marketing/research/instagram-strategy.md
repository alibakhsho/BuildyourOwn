## 2. Content Types That Work

### Ranked by Engagement (Highest to Lowest)

#### 🏆 Tier 1: Highest Engagement (5–10%+ engagement rate)

**1. Before & After Transformations**
- Format: Carousel (swipe to reveal) or Reel with transition effect
- Why: Dopamine hit from dramatic visual change; highly shareable
- Best practice: First slide = "Before" with text overlay showing budget; Last slide = stunning "After"
- Save rate: 3–5x higher than standard posts
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** "This homeowner estimated their kitchen reno at $45K using our AI. Here's the result 👇"

**2. Time-Lapse Build/Renovation Videos**
- Format: 15–30 second Reels
- Why: Satisfying, mesmerising content — high completion rates
- Best practice: Wide-angle, sped up 8–16x, add trending audio
- Share rate: Highest of any construction content type
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** Partner with builders to film renos, overlay the estimate vs actual cost

**3. Cost Breakdown Posts**
- Format: Carousel (infographic style) or text-heavy Reel
- Why: Extremely high save rate — people bookmark for planning
- Save rate: 5–8x higher than average posts
- Best practice: Itemised cost breakdowns by room/project, include regional variations (AU/US/UK)
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** "What a bathroom reno ACTUALLY costs in 2026" → drives app downloads for detailed estimates

#### 🥈 Tier 2: Strong Engagement (3–5%)

**4. Educational Tips / "Things I Wish I Knew"**
- Format: Carousel (slide-by-slide tips) or talking-head Reel
- Why: Positions brand as authority; high save + share rates
- Topics that work: "5 mistakes first-time renovators make", "What your builder won't tell you", "How to read a building plan"
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** "How to read construction plans like a pro (or let AI do it for you)"

**5. "Is This Worth It?" / Budget Reality Checks**
- Format: Reel or carousel comparing expectations vs reality
- Why: Taps into financial anxiety around renovations; drives comments
- Topics: "Is a $30K kitchen reno realistic?", "What $100K gets you in each state"
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** Direct CTA to try the free estimator

**6. Material & Product Comparisons**
- Format: Carousel (side-by-side) or Reel with samples
- Why: Practical, reference-worthy content that gets saved
- Topics: "Engineered vs hardwood flooring", "Quartz vs granite countertops"
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** Show how material choices affect the total estimate in the app

#### 🥉 Tier 3: Solid Engagement (2–3%)

**7. Behind-the-Scenes / Day-in-the-Life**
- Format: Stories (primary) and Reels
- Why: Builds personal connection and trust
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** "Day in the life of a renovation planner using AI"

**8. Tool & Tech Reviews**
- Format: Short Reels, demo-style
- Why: Tradies love gear content; homeowners love knowing what pros use
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** App walkthroughs disguised as "tool reviews"

**9. Client/User Testimonials & Case Studies**
- Format: Carousel with project photos + quote overlay, or short interview Reel
- Why: Social proof converts; builders sharing positive tool experiences
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** "How [Builder Name] quoted 3x faster with BuildYourOwn"

**10. Memes & Relatable Humour**
- Format: Static image or short Reel
- Why: High share rate; reaches new audiences via reshares
- Topics: Renovation fails, budget blowout jokes, "contractor said 2 weeks" memes
- **BuildYourOwn angle:** Relatable renovation humour with subtle branding

| Content Type | % of Posts | Primary Format |

## 5. Reels vs Static vs Carousel Performance

### Platform-Wide Benchmarks (2025-2026 data)

| Metric | Reels | Carousels | Static Images |
|--------|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Avg Reach** | Highest (2–3x static) | High (1.5–2x static) | Baseline |
| **Avg Engagement Rate** | 1.23% | 1.92% | 0.70% |
| **Saves** | Moderate | Highest | Low |
| **Shares** | Highest | Moderate | Low |
| **Comments** | Moderate | Highest | Moderate |
| **Follower Growth** | Highest | Moderate | Low |
| **Algorithm Priority** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ⭐⭐ |

**Key stat:** Reels account for **46% of time spent on Instagram** and are shared more than **4.5 billion times per day** (Hootsuite/Meta, 2026).

### Construction/Renovation Niche Performance

#### Reels (Best for: Reach & Follower Growth)
- **Time-lapse builds:** 15–30 sec Reels get **3–5x the reach** of static posts
- **Before/after transitions:** Using trending transition effects = viral potential
- **Talking-head tips:** "3 things to know before renovating" format performs well with **2–4% engagement**
- **App demos:** Screen recordings with voiceover — lower engagement but **highest conversion to app install**
- **Optimal length:** 15–30 seconds for max reach; 60–90 seconds for education/depth
- **Construction-specific insight:** Satisfying process videos (tiling, plastering, painting) are Instagram's "oddly satisfying" niche — extremely high share rate

#### Carousels (Best for: Engagement & Saves — THE WORKHORSE)
- **Highest engagement format** in the renovation niche by a significant margin
- **Cost breakdown carousels** get **3–5x more saves** than any other format
- **"Swipe for the after" carousels** drive the highest comment rates
- **Educational tip carousels** (5–7 slides) are the most bookmarked content type
- **Optimal slides:** 7–10 slides (users who start swiping tend to finish)
- **Construction-specific insight:** "Budget breakdown" carousels (Slide 1: total budget, Slides 2–8: room-by-room costs, Slide 9: CTA) consistently outperform
- **Design tip:** Use consistent branded templates with bold text overlays

#### Static Images (Best for: Brand Aesthetics & Portfolio)
- **Declining in reach** — Instagram's algorithm de-prioritises static content
- **Still useful for:** Professional portfolio shots, completed project showcases, announcement posts
- **Construction-specific insight:** High-quality photography of finished renovations still performs if stunning
- **Recommendation:** Limit to 10–15% of content; use primarily for grid aesthetics

### Format Recommendations for BuildYourOwn

| Format | % of Content | Best Use Cases |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Reels** | 50% | Time-lapses, app demos, tips, transitions, trending audio |
| **Carousels** | 35% | Cost breakdowns, educational tips, before/after, testimonials |
| **Static** | 10% | Finished project showcases, announcements |
| **Stories** | Daily | Behind-scenes, polls, Q&As, link stickers to app |

### Reel-Specific Best Practices for Construction
1. **Hook in first 1.5 seconds** — open with the dramatic "after" or a bold statement
2. **Use trending audio** — even construction content benefits from trending sounds
3. **Add captions/text overlays** — 80%+ watch without sound
4. **Include clear CTA** — "Link in bio to estimate your reno" in final frame
5. **Leverage "series" format** — "Renovation Cost Series: Episode 1 — Kitchens"
6. **Post Reels at peak times** — Reels posted during high-activity windows get 2x algorithmic boost

## 6. Ad Formats That Convert for SaaS/App Installs

### Meta Ad Formats Ranked by Construction App Performance

#### 🏆 1. Reels Ads (Highest Performer for App Installs)
- **CPI (Cost Per Install):** $1.50–$4.00 (construction niche)
- **Why it works:** Full-screen, immersive, feels native to feed
- **Best creative approach:**
  - 15-second screen recording of app in action (plan upload → AI reading → 3D visualization → estimate)
  - UGC-style: "I can't believe this app is free" format
  - Problem/solution: "Tired of getting quotes that are 3x over budget?"
- **Targeting:** Interest-based (home renovation, construction, DIY) + lookalike audiences
- **BuildYourOwn creative ideas:**
  - "Watch AI read this building plan in 5 seconds" (curiosity hook)
  - "Free app that tells you exactly what your reno will cost" (value prop)
  - "Builders are using this to quote 3x faster" (tradie angle)

#### 🥈 2. Carousel Ads (Best for Explaining Features)
- **CPI:** $2.00–$5.00
- **Why it works:** Each card showcases a different feature; users self-select interest
- **Best creative approach:**
  - Card 1: Hook/problem ("Planning a renovation?")
  - Card 2: AI Plan Reading feature
  - Card 3: 3D Visualization feature
  - Card 4: Cost Estimation feature
  - Card 5: "6 AI Specialists" feature
  - Card 6: CTA ("Download Free — No Login Required")
- **Key differentiator:** "No login required" and "Free" should be prominent — reduces friction anxiety

#### 🥉 3. Story Ads (High Volume, Lower CPM)
- **CPI:** $2.00–$4.50
- **Why it works:** Full-screen, quick consumption, swipe-up CTA
- **Best creative approach:**
  - 5–10 second video showing the app's most impressive feature (3D visualization)
  - Before/after: "This homeowner used BuildYourOwn to plan their $60K reno"
  - Urgency/curiosity: "Most people overpay 30% on renovations. Here's how to stop."

#### 4. In-Feed Video Ads
- **CPI:** $2.50–$6.00
- **Best for:** Retargeting warm audiences who visited website/engaged with content
- **Creative:** 30–60 second product walkthrough with social proof

#### 5. Explore Ads
- **CPI:** $3.00–$7.00
- **Why it works:** Catches users in discovery mode — already browsing renovation content
- **Best for:** Broad awareness campaigns targeting home renovation interest

### Ad Creative Best Practices for Construction App

#### What Converts
1. **Screen recordings showing the app in action** — most effective single creative type
2. **"This free app..." hooks** — combining "free" + "app" + surprising capability
3. **UGC/creator-style ads** — person talking to camera about the app (3–5x better than polished brand ads)
4. **Problem-agitation-solution format:**
   - "Getting renovation quotes is painful" (problem)
   - "You call 5 builders, wait 3 weeks, get wildly different numbers" (agitation)
   - "BuildYourOwn gives you an AI estimate in minutes — free, no login" (solution)
5. **Social proof numbers** — "Join 50K+ homeowners who've estimated their reno" (once you have the numbers)

#### What Doesn't Convert
- Overly polished/corporate brand videos
- Feature lists without showing the actual app
- Stock footage of construction sites
- Long-form ads (>60 seconds) for cold audiences

### Targeting Strategy

#### Audience Segments
| Segment | Targeting | Est. Audience Size |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Homeowners planning renos** | Interest: Home improvement, Renovation, Interior Design, Houzz, The Block | 10M+ (AU/US/UK) |
| **First-home buyers** | Interest: First home buyer, Real estate + Age 25–40 | 5M+ |
| **Tradies/Builders** | Interest: Construction, Building materials, Trade tools, Carpentry | 3M+ |
| **Architects/Designers** | Job title: Architect, Interior Designer, Building Designer | 500K+ |
| **Lookalike audiences** | Based on app installers / high-value users | Variable |
| **Retargeting** | Website visitors, video viewers (75%+), engaged followers | Variable |

#### Campaign Structure
1. **Top of Funnel (Awareness):** Reels ads + Explore ads — broad targeting, optimise for reach
2. **Middle of Funnel (Consideration):** Carousel ads showing features — interest-based targeting
3. **Bottom of Funnel (Conversion):** Retargeting video ads — app install CTA, optimise for installs
4. **Retention:** Story ads to existing users — new feature announcements

### Budget Allocation Recommendation
| Campaign Phase | % of Budget | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness (Reels/Explore) | 40% | Reach renovation-interested audiences |
| Consideration (Carousel/Video) | 30% | Feature education, app store visits |
| Conversion (Retargeting) | 20% | App installs from warm audiences |
| Retention/Upsell | 10% | Feature adoption, reviews |

### Key Metrics to Track
- **CPI (Cost Per Install):** Target < $3.00 AU, < $2.50 US, < $2.00 UK
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Aim for > 1.5% (industry avg ~1%)
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Track through to premium feature adoption
- **Thumb-Stop Rate:** % who pause on your ad (aim > 25%)
- **Video View Rate (75%+):** Indicates strong creative — retarget these viewers

## 7. BuildYourOwn-Specific Recommendations

### Unique Selling Points to Emphasise on Instagram
1. **"Free, No Login Required"** — This is your #1 conversion driver. Most competitors require signup. Lead with this.
2. **AI Plan Reading** — Show this in every app demo. It's visually impressive and unique.
3. **3D Visualization** — Eye candy for Instagram. Before/after 2D plan → 3D render is perfect Reel content.
4. **6 AI Specialist Personas** — Create a "meet the team" content series for each AI persona.
5. **Instant Estimates** — Speed is compelling. "Get your estimate in under 60 seconds."

### Content Series Ideas
1. **"What Does It Cost?" Series** — Weekly cost breakdowns by project type (kitchen, bathroom, extension, deck, etc.) with regional pricing (AU/US/UK)
2. **"Reno Reality Check" Series** — Compare budgets to actual costs, debunk renovation myths
3. **"AI vs Human" Series** — Race the AI against a human estimator (entertaining + demonstrates product)
4. **"Plan Reading in 30 Seconds" Series** — Upload different plans, show AI reading them live
5. **"Meet Your AI Specialist" Series** — Introduce each of the 6 AI personas with their expertise
6. **"Tradie Tuesday" Series** — Tips specifically for builders on quoting faster

### Growth Tactics
1. **Collaborate with renovation influencers** — Give them the app, film them using it for their project
2. **Partner with The Block (AU) contestants/fans** — Huge engaged audience
3. **Create a "Reno Calculator" AR filter** — Interactive filter that drives profile visits
4. **Run a "Best Renovation" contest** — User submissions with app estimates for social proof
5. **Cross-post Reels to Facebook** — Same content, 2x the reach (Meta's cross-platform boost)
6. **Engage in renovation community hashtags** — Comment genuinely on #homerenovation posts
7. **Feature user renovations** — Repost (with permission) user projects estimated with BuildYourOwn

### Quick Wins (First 30 Days)
1. Set up business profile with clear bio: "Free AI renovation estimator 🏠 No login needed | AU 🇦🇺 US 🇺🇸 UK 🇬🇧"
2. Create 10 cost breakdown carousels (by room type × region)
3. Record 5 app demo Reels (plan upload, 3D viz, estimate generation)
4. Post 3x/week minimum, 1 Reel + 1 Carousel + 1 flexible format
5. Use Stories daily with polls: "What's your renovation budget?" / "Which room would you renovate first?"
6. Engage with 20+ accounts daily in renovation hashtags
7. Set up Meta ads with $20–50/day budget testing 3 Reel ad creatives
8. Create a Linktree or link-in-bio with: App download, Cost guides, Contact
