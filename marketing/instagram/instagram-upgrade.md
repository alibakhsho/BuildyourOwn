# Instagram upgrade: the whole app, in video

This sits on top of `week-plan-homeowners-3d.md` (the posting calendar). It covers the profile, the look of the grid, one video per part of the software, and the prompts for photoreal clips.

## 1. Profile

- **Name:** BuildYourOwn | See & price your build
- **Bio:**
  See your house in 3D in seconds 🏠
  Know what it costs before you call a builder
  Free for homeowners · built for tradies & builders
  👇 Try it on your own place
- **Highlights (in this order):** Start here · 3D · Cost · Plan reader · AI crew · For builders
- **Pinned posts (3):** `build` reel · `plan3d` reel · the "Where every dollar goes" carousel

## 2. The look

Every post uses the same frame, so the grid reads as one brand:
- Daylight background (`#EEF1EE`), ink text (`#14171A`), orange eyebrow (`#C2570A`), hi-vis yellow for the active thing (`#F5C518`).
- Small orange eyebrow, then a headline of 2–4 words. One white card at the bottom holds the number.
- The 3D house is always the real model from the app.
- Alternate on the grid: animated 3D reel → photoreal clip → carousel.

## 4. Photoreal clips (Higgsfield)

Use these between the 3D reels for the "realistic" look. Settings for all: **9:16, 5–8s, 1080p, audio off**. To keep the house the same as the app's, upload a frame from `build.mp4` (the finished house) as the **start image** and add the prompt.

1. **Hero reveal.** "Photorealistic two-storey red brick Australian family home with dark Colorbond roof, solar panels and an attached garage, golden hour, slow cinematic drone push-in from the street, soft long shadows, shallow depth of field, real estate film look."
2. **Time-lapse build.** "Photoreal construction time-lapse of the same house: concrete slab, timber frame rising, roof trusses, brick walls going up course by course, finished home. Locked-off wide shot, clouds racing, tradies moving fast, natural daylight."
3. **Homeowner moment.** "A couple at a kitchen table at night looking at a phone showing a 3D model of a house, warm lamp light, relieved smiles, handheld documentary feel, shallow depth of field."
4. **Builder on site.** "Australian builder in hi-vis and a hard hat on a timber-frame house site holding a tablet showing a construction schedule, morning light, slow dolly-in, documentary style."
5. **Plan to real.** "Top-down shot of a hand-drawn floor plan on a wooden desk; the lines lift off the paper and grow into a miniature photoreal house standing on the plan; macro lens, soft daylight."
6. **Keys.** "Family walking up the path to the front door of the same finished brick home, late afternoon sun, camera follows from behind at waist height, gentle lens flare."

Each clip gets the same eyebrow + headline frame from section 2 added in Instagram or CapCut.
