# ChatGPT Ads / AI Platform Advertising

## ChatGPT Sponsored Results (OpenAI Ads)

### How It Works
OpenAI launched sponsored results in ChatGPT in 2025-2026. When users ask questions related to your product category, your ad can appear as a sponsored suggestion within the conversation.

### Targeting Strategy
**Trigger queries (when someone asks ChatGPT):**
- "How much does it cost to build a house?"
- "How do I estimate renovation costs?"
- "What's a good construction estimating tool?"
- "How to create a building quote?"
- "What should a kitchen renovation cost?"

### Ad Format
```
[Sponsored]
BuildYourOwn — Free AI Construction Estimator
Get instant, itemised construction costs from dimensions or a plan photo.
Six AI specialists included. No signup needed.
→ Try free at buildyourown.app
```

### ChatGPT Plugin / GPT Integration
**Alternative approach:** Build a custom GPT or ChatGPT plugin that connects to BuildYourOwn's API:
- User asks "How much would a 3-bed house extension cost in Sydney?"
- GPT calls BuildYourOwn API → returns real estimate
- Links to full interactive experience

**This is potentially more powerful than ads** — it makes BuildYourOwn the answer engine for construction cost queries inside ChatGPT.
