# BuildYourOwn — Brand Positioning & Strategy

## The Big Idea
**"Professional construction estimating, without needing to be a professional."**

BuildYourOwn sits in an empty space: **consumer-simple, contractor-serious**. Every other construction tool forces you to pick a side — either it's dumbed-down Houzz-style "inspiration" with no real numbers, or it's Procore/Buildxact that takes 6 weeks to learn and costs $400/month. We're neither. We're the tool a homeowner uses to understand their reno before calling a builder — and the same tool that builder uses to quote the job in 20 minutes instead of 3 days.

---

## Brand Pillars

### 1. Instant Clarity
> "Know what it costs before you call anyone."

- No signup. No paywall on the estimate.
- Type dimensions → see numbers, 3D, materials list. Live.
- Upload a plan photo → AI reads rooms, doors, windows in seconds.

### 2. AI Crew, Not a Chatbot
> "Six specialists in your pocket. Each one thinks differently."

- Marcus the site veteran challenges your buildability.
- Priya the QS audits your numbers.
- Chen flags what needs an engineer.
- Sofia picks finishes that fit your budget.
- David plans the critical path.
- Amara bundles your orders and finds savings.

### 3. From Quote to Claim
> "Don't just estimate — manage the build."

- Win the job → track budget vs committed → raise POs → claim progress.
- Push drafts straight to Xero or MYOB.
- Takeoff quantities from a photo of the plan.

### 4. Built for the Site, Not the Boardroom
> "No training manual. If you can use your phone, you can use this."

- Works on a phone at the kitchen table or a laptop in the site shed.
- Plain English. Australian/US/UK rates built in.
- 3D model rebuilds the moment you move a slider.

## Target Segments

### Segment 1: Homeowners Planning a Build/Reno (Primary)
- **Who:** 25–55, owns or buying a home, planning a reno ($20k–$500k), renovation or new build
- **Pain:** No idea what things cost. Gets 3 quotes that vary by 40%. Can't tell if they're being ripped off.
- **Job to do:** Understand costs before committing. Walk into a meeting with a builder already informed.
- **Hook:** "Know what your kitchen reno should cost — in 60 seconds."

### Segment 2: Residential Builders & Tradies (Secondary → Co-Primary)
- **Who:** 1–20 person building companies, sole trader chippies/sparkies, subbies
- **Pain:** Quoting takes 2–3 days per job. Half the quotes don't convert. Admin is killing them.
- **Job to do:** Quote faster, win more work, stop losing money on scope creep.
- **Hook:** "Stop losing weekends to takeoffs. Upload the plan, AI reads it, quote goes out Monday."

### Segment 3: Property Developers & Investors
- **Who:** Small developers doing 1–5 projects/year, investors evaluating feasibility
- **Pain:** Need quick feasibility numbers before engaging consultants
- **Job to do:** Know if a site/project stacks up financially in 10 minutes
- **Hook:** "High-rise feasibility in 10 minutes. Floor plate, structure type, and a number you can take to the bank."

## Voice & Tone

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct** | Say it plainly. No jargon unless it's trade jargon they already know. | "Your roof costs $14,200" not "Estimated roofing expenditure: $14,200" |
| **Confident** | We know construction. The numbers are real. | "Based on 2024 AU rates" not "We think maybe..." |
| **Warm** | We're on their side, not selling to them. | "Let's see what that deck actually costs" |
| **Technical when needed** | Tradies respect competence. Don't dumb it down for them. | "MGP10 studs at 600 centres, plates & lintels" |

## Color Palette (from existing design system)

- **Hi-vis Orange** (#F97316) — CTAs, key data, emphasis. Construction hi-vis.
- **Dark Charcoal** — Background, professionalism.
- **White/Light** — Text, cards, clean contrast.
- The "hi-vis on dark" look is unique in ConTech — most competitors use bland blue/white SaaS palettes. Keep it.
